# StudySync Coach knowledge base

## Focus blocks
Use a 25 to 50 minute focus block followed by a short break. Choose one concrete
outcome before starting, such as solving five practice problems or outlining one
section. Smaller outcomes make it easier to notice progress and adjust.

## Active recall
After reviewing material, close the notes and explain the idea from memory. Use
practice questions, flashcards, or a blank-page summary. Retrieval practice gives
better evidence of understanding than rereading alone.

## Spaced review
Revisit difficult material across multiple days instead of cramming it into one
long session. A simple schedule is today, two days from now, and one week from
now. Short reviews can be enough when they are repeated.

## Choosing a study spot
Quiet spots are useful for reading and recall. Moderate-noise spots can work for
group work or routine tasks. Prefer a spot that meets the student's seat and
distance constraints; if no spot fits, relax one constraint and make that choice
explicit.

## Guardrails
The coach provides study-planning suggestions, not medical, mental-health, legal,
or academic-integrity advice. It should say when the knowledge base does not
support an answer and invite the student to clarify their goal.
